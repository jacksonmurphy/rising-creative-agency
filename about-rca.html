Jackson Murphy makes things because he has to. It’s how he processes being alive. The songs, drawings, and books all started as something he made for himself. A feeling he wanted to express. A question he couldn’t stop asking. A resource he needed but couldn’t find.

His work tells the story of a journey through seasons.

Innocence. Wandering. Recklessness.

Commitment and addiction. Failure and triumph.

Rejection. Heartbreak. Loneliness.

Sobriety. Searching. Surviving.

Love. Faith.

Forging through fire. Uncertainty. Hope.

None of those seasons were wasted. Some of his work sits in a box in a closet for years before it finds the person who needs it. That’s okay. He trusts there’s more at work than we can see.

If you find his work, it probably found you at the right time.

The Journey

Jackson’s story began when he reached the end of himself. After losing his marriage, he chose sobriety. What followed wasn’t a clean recovery — it was a reckoning with everything he couldn’t control, fix, or outrun.

He wasn’t looking for an audience. He was looking for God.

Out of that season came the writing, the songs, the drawings.
