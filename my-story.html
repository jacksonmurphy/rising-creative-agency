Where it began

Loss, sobriety, and a deeper return to self.

After losing his marriage, Jackson made the decision to get sober. He is now more than four years sober, and that decision became the foundation of everything that followed.

What came next was not a straight line. It was a long season of heartbreak, uncertainty, spiritual searching, attachment work, journaling, and learning how to stay present when life did not offer easy answers.

During that time, writing became more than expression. It became a way to listen, to process, and to begin rebuilding a life from the inside out.

What the work became

Not product-first. Process-first.

The journals, parables, songs, and reflections were not created as a brand strategy first. They emerged from a lived process of healing and spiritual reorientation.

Rise and Shine became guided companions for reflection. Sacred Communication grew out of trying to understand how love, safety, surrender, and nervous-system peace interact. The parables became symbolic ways to explore healing, hope, grief, trust, and restoration.
