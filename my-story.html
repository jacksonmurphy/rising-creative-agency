My Story

Where it began

This started in a season that broke me open.

Not all at once, but gradually through experiences that challenged what I thought I knew about love, identity, and direction. Four years ago, I lost my marriage and made the decision to get sober. I’m now more than four years sober, but sobriety was only the beginning of the journey.

During that time, I found myself writing more. Reflecting. Sitting with things instead of trying to solve them. There wasn’t a plan behind it. Just a desire to understand, to heal, and to stay grounded through heartbreak, uncertainty, and change.

Over time, something began to take shape. I started noticing patterns in what I was writing about: learning how to stay present instead of rushing ahead, letting go of control one step at a time, and finding peace without needing everything to be certain.

It didn’t feel like I was creating something new. It felt more like I was uncovering something that had been there all along.

The Turning Point

A simple reflection

This didn’t start as a program. It started as a process: learning how to stay grounded in love, faith, and uncertainty.

The journals helped me slow down when everything felt overwhelming. The parables gave pain a deeper meaning. The songs gave emotion somewhere to go. And Sacred Communication became a quieter way of relating to myself, to others, and to life.

Sacred Communication

What Sacred Communication became.

Over time, I began to see something consistent: peace wasn’t coming from having answers. It was coming from how I showed up — with presence, honesty, and a willingness to let things unfold.

View the Sacred Communication page

Presence before persuasion

Love becomes safer when it is not trying to convince, fix, or control. Presence calms before words explain.

Blessing before intensity

Healing begins when we speak life gently, honor wounds without trying to remove them, and let compassion lead.

Surrender before certainty

Some battles are not won through force. They are given over to God, one breath, one boundary, and one act of trust at a time.

A

What it means

Sacred Communication is not a technique. It is a spiritual posture rooted in safety, reverence, and holy love. It was born out of silence, the kind that brings you face-to-face with God — and taught me that peace can be part of love too.

Why it matters

If any part of this connects with where you are, you are welcome to explore it in your own way. This framework grew out of learning how to love with patience instead of pressure, and how to stay grounded without needing everything to be resolved.

The First 7 Days

This is what the reset looked like in real time.

These are actual pages from the journal, along with the Daily Manifestation that set the tone for the week. Not after the fact. Not polished. Just the beginning of a practice that helped me stay grounded in the middle of uncertainty.

Daily Manifestation

The opening intention for the week: to show up with gratitude, align with truth, and begin a new chapter with an open heart.

“Today begins a new chapter — and I am ready.”

Day 1 · Begin again

I welcomed a new beginning with an open heart and gentle expectations. I reminded myself that clarity unfolds as I walk, not before.

“Today, I begin again — not empty, but full. Full of lessons. Full of love. Full of quiet strength.”

Day 2 · Trust the next step

I did not need the whole path. I only needed the next honest step. I practiced quiet faith instead of urgency.

“I trust that what is meant for me is meeting me along the way. Every step I take leads me closer to who I am becoming.”

Day 3 · Nurture what is becoming

I honored the dreams within me, letting growth remain quiet, steady, and real. I moved through the day grounded and hopeful.

“Nothing is late. Nothing is missing. What is meant for me is finding me.”

Day 4 · Honor your words

I chose language that blesses instead of rushes. I wanted my words to create healing, courage, and growth.

“I move through this day aware, grounded, and open — speaking life into each moment as it comes.”

Day 5 · Return to your breath

I slowed down enough to welcome calm, release what no longer served me, and trust that clarity arrives when I listen.

“With each breath, I rise into peace, purpose, and presence.”

Day 6 · Loosen your grip

I released control disguised as care and chose trust instead. What is for me does not require force.

“What I send out with love returns in ways I couldn’t plan. I am held. I am guided.”

Day 7 · Choose hope

I chose hope not as an escape, but as a strength. Even when the path feels steep, hope keeps me moving.

“I rise with hope, even when the path is steep.”
