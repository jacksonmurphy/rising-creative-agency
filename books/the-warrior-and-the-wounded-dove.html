About the Book

The Warrior and The Wounded Dove: A Parable of Faith, Healing, and Surrender
Kindle Edition
by Jackson Murphy (Author)

In a kingdom shaped by rivers and seasons, a weary warrior learns that the fiercest battles are not fought with swords, but within the heart.

When a wounded dove enters his life, he offers her safety, patience, and gentleness. But when fear stirs old memories, she flies away. The warrior is left with a choice: to chase, or to trust.

This illustrated parable explores what love becomes when control is released and trust is placed in God’s hands.

Inside you will find a poetic story of faith and emotional healing, hand-illustrated artwork that mirrors surrender, and a message of hope for seasons of waiting, loss, or transformation.

This is not a story about winning love back. It is a story about becoming whole.
