About the Book

The Dove, the Warrior, and the Healer: A Parable of Grace, Love, and Restoration
Paperback - April 1, 2026
by Jackson Murphy (Author)

In a quiet valley between two rivers lives a warrior, strong, faithful, and weary from battles no one else can see. One day, a dove enters his world carrying more than wings were meant to hold.

What begins as a simple act of care slowly becomes something far deeper. But when an unexpected wound sends the dove into the wilderness, both the warrior and the dove must walk their own paths of healing.

Along the way, they encounter the quiet work of the Healer, who restores broken wings, reveals hidden wounds of the heart, and teaches that love cannot be forced or protected by strength alone.

This illustrated parable explores the weight we carry for others, the fear of letting go, the courage it takes to heal, and the grace it takes to trust restoration.
