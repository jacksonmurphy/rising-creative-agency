About the Book

The Shepherd Who Calls You by Name: A Sacred Space of Being Found and Restored
Paperback - March 31, 2026
by Jackson Murphy (Author)

The Shepherd Who Calls You by Name is a quiet, contemplative story of wandering, being found, and learning to rest again.

Told through a simple yet deeply moving parable, this book follows a lamb who drifts from the flock, not out of rebellion, but distraction, and slowly loses the sound of the Shepherd's voice.

As the world grows unfamiliar and fear settles in, the lamb forgets not only where he belongs, but who he belongs to. But even in the silence, something remains. A presence draws near. Not loudly. Not forcefully. But steadily, gently... and by name.

Through soft, minimalist storytelling and hand-drawn imagery, this book invites readers into a sacred space of being seen, carried, and restored.
