About the Book

The House the Architect Restored: A Parable of Healing, Courage, and Restoration
Paperback - April 1, 2026
by Jackson Murphy (Author)

A contemplative illustrated parable about restoration, healing, and the quiet courage to rebuild what once felt broken.

In a quiet valley stands a house long abandoned, its roof fallen, its walls worn, and its foundation weakened by time. Many pass by, believing it too damaged to restore.

But one woman sees something different. Where others see ruin, she sees beauty waiting to be uncovered.

Choosing to stay, she begins the slow, often painful work of restoration. Along the way, she meets an Architect who offers guidance, truth, and a path forward if she is willing to do the work.

Piece by piece, what was once broken becomes strong. What was once dark fills with light. What was once abandoned becomes a place of warmth, life, and quiet testimony.
