About the Journal

A gentle, structured 90 days.

RISE is a companion, a mirror, and a transformational tool designed to brighten your inner world and rewrite your story from the inside out.

Through a blend of prompts, encouraging guidance, and reflective space, this 90-day journey invites you to cultivate gratitude, speak to your desires with love, align with abundance, and release old emotional patterns.

Created by author and storyteller Jackson Murphy, this journal was born from a personal journey of healing, faith, emotional rebuilding, and the power of manifestation. Each page is crafted to help you shift perspective, uncover clarity, and live from the heart with intention.

Every day brings a moment of reflection, space to record your growth, and inspiration to keep moving forward — no matter what season you’re walking through.
