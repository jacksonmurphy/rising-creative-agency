About the Companion

Not a book about hustle.

Shine is a book about alignment — a 90-day guided reflection companion to help you slow down, soften inward, and step gently into the next version of yourself.

Through themes of courage, healing, belief, clarity, gratitude, resilience, and self-trust, Shine walks you through the quiet work of transformation — not in dramatic leaps, but in steady becoming.

Each day offers a powerful guiding word, a grounding affirmation, a thoughtful reflection, space to write and respond, and a reminder that change happens in small, loving choices.

Shine is best experienced alongside RISE: 90 Days to a Brighter You — but it also stands beautifully on its own.
