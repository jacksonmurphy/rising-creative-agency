SHINE
A 90-Day Reflection Companion to RISE

What if growth didn’t require becoming someone new… but remembering who you’ve always been?

This is not a book about hustle. It is a book about alignment.

Each day offers:

  • A powerful guiding word
  • A grounding affirmation
  • A thoughtful reflection
  • Space to write and respond
  • A reminder that change happens in small, loving choices

Through themes of courage, healing, belief, clarity, gratitude, resilience, and self-trust, SHINE walks you through the quiet work of transformation — not in dramatic leaps, but in steady becoming.

SHINE is best experienced alongside RISE: 90 Days to a Brighter You — but it also stands beautifully on its own.

This is not the end of your journey. This is your next beginning.
