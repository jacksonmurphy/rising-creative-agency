About Jackson

Where it began

Jackson’s story began in a season that broke him open. After losing his marriage, he made the decision to get sober. Sobriety became the beginning of a deeper journey through heartbreak, uncertainty, faith, and self-reckoning.

During that season, he started writing more. Reflecting. Sitting with what he could not solve. Over time, the journals, parables, and songs became a way to stay grounded in love, faith, and uncertainty.

What began as a personal process eventually became books, music, and guided experiences that now live on Rising Creative Agency.

Why the work exists

Not a product first. A process first.

Rise, Shine, the parables, and Sacred Communication all came out of the same journey: learning how to begin again without pressure or perfection.
